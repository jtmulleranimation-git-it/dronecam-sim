Takes

Each take is a dronecam-path file, the same format your Unreal recorder writes. Share / Save sends it with AirDrop or to Files (on PC it downloads). Copy it into Saved/DronePaths in your project to replay it with the Drone Path Player. The car's path is saved in the same file.

Follow in Cine / Normal / Sport obeys the camera-drone speed limits (a real drone falls behind a 130 km/h car). Use FPV, or untick "Follow obeys…" in Settings, to keep up.

Scoring

Points every second the car is in view: crosshair on the car, the car filling more of the frame (cropped parts don't count), and nice composition (car on a rule-of-thirds point with room ahead of it). Flying close adds up to ×1.6, and holding a good frame builds a combo up to ×3. Lock-on assists cost points: Follow ×0.4, Look At ×0.7. Penalties: car −500, trees / gate posts −150, road or ground −200 (takeoff and gentle landings are free). The 16:9 guide goes from grey to green as your crosshair closes in, and is solid green on the car. It's only a visual aid and can be turned off.
